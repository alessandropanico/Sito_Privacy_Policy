Manga Chronicles Privacy Policy

Effective Date: December 5, 2025

1. Information We Collect

Manga Chronicles collects personal information necessary to provide and improve our services. This data is collected when you create an account, use the application, or interact with its features:

  • Account Identifiers:
    • Email Address: Required for account creation, login, and security purposes (e.g., password recovery).
    • Hashed Password: Your password is encrypted (hashed) and stored securely for authentication. We do not store or have access to your plain-text password.
    • Display Name (Nickname): Used to personalize your experience and display your created stories.
    • Profile Image (Avatar): Custom-uploaded by the user.
  • User-Generated Content & AI Input Data:
    • Custom Profile Banner: Image uploaded to personalize your profile banner.
    • Story and Character Content: Text and metadata of stories and characters you create.
    • AI Prompts: Prompts and inputs used to generate **AI Covers, Character Art, and AI Story Text (racconti)**, stored to allow you to regenerate or manage your content. **Questi prompt possono essere elaborati da fornitori di AI di terze parti (come specificato in Sezione 3).**
  • Usage and Activity Data: Information about stories created, features accessed (e.g., AI generation usage), timestamps, and current Ear-Coins balance displayed in your profile.
  • Advertising Identifiers (AdMob): Non-personal device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID or Identifier for Advertisers) used by Google AdMob for ad targeting and performance measurement.

2. How We Use Your Information (Purposes & Legal Basis - GDPR)

  • To manage your account and provide access to the application (Legal Basis: Performance of a Contract).
  • To store your generated content (profile banner, stories, characters, AI prompts) on Firebase (Legal Basis: Performance of a Contract).
  • To **generate new text stories and content** based on your input using third-party AI models (Legal Basis: **Performance of a Contract**).
  • To track and display your Ear-Coins balance in your profile, and enable the reward system for ads and in-app purchases (Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest / Consent).
  • To monetize the service through advertisements (AdMob) and **manage access to certain features, inclusa la generazione di storie AI, tramite annunci con premio** (Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest / Consent).
  • To communicate with you regarding service updates, account information, or security issues (Legal Basis: Legitimate Interest).

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Data Processors)

  • Firebase (Google LLC) - Hosting and Backend: Provides secure authentication (Firebase Auth), database services (Firestore/Realtime Database), and file storage (Firebase Storage) for user profiles, story data, AI-generated images, and Ear-Coins balances.
  • Google Generative AI (es. Gemini) - Content Generation: Utilizzato per processare i **prompt di testo e genere** e generare il contenuto delle storie AI e le descrizioni per le cover. Gli input vengono inviati ai modelli di AI di Google.

    Google gestisce i dati secondo le sue policy e li utilizza per mantenere e migliorare i suoi servizi, ma non utilizza i prompt degli utenti per l'addestramento dei modelli, a meno che non sia specificato diversamente nelle API terms.

  • Google AdMob - Advertising Services: Used to display rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob collects device identifiers and approximate location data to serve ads and track performance. **Questi annunci sono utilizzati anche come "gating" (sblocco) per accedere a funzionalità ad alto costo computazionale come la generazione di storie AI.**

    AdMob Consent: By using the service, you acknowledge that Google may use data about your app usage and other apps to serve relevant ads. You may opt-out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

4. Data Retention and Security

  • Retention: Personal data (email, hashed password, nickname, content, **AI prompts,** Ear-Coins balance) is retained while your account is active. Upon account deletion request, data will be removed within 30 days, subject to legal obligations.
  • Security: Firebase security features (secure authentication, Firestore Security Rules, password hashing) are implemented to protect your data. Absolute security cannot be guaranteed due to inherent internet risks.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to access, rectify, port, erase, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email below. Note: AdMob data and Generative AI prompt data are processed by Google, and such requests may need to be directed to Google as per applicable law.
